Hi, I am Anne-Hélène,
I'm a front-end developer!

I've been working independently since 2017, building and customizing websites for small businesses, cultural venues and agencies. I'm now focusing on React, with the goal of joining a product team.

How I got here ?

I started my career in customer service and product management at Filofax, where I led after-sales and customer support and relaunched the Yard-O-LED brand (sales doubled in 12 months).
In parallel, I completed a Master's degree in Marketing at the CNAM, studying in the evenings.

In 2014, after moving into e-marketing, I discovered web development and completed Le Wagon's 9-week intensive bootcamp in Paris, learning Ruby on Rails, JavaScript and Git. I then worked in technical customer support at Multiposting (SAP), handling web services, XML feeds and API-related issues for large accounts.

Since 2017, I've been working independently, combining client projects (WordPress, e-commerce, custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript, SEO) with teaching: I taught HTML/CSS in the Master's program Cultures et Métiers du web at Université Gustave Eiffel (2024–2025).

How I keep growing

I follow Scrimba's Frontend Developer Path to deepen my skills in modern JavaScript (ES6+), React, responsive design, accessibility and best practices.

The projects showcased on this portfolio were built while following Scrimba's courses: they are my way of putting new concepts into practice. For each one, I've added my own touches ([e.g. my own design, deployment, API integration, accessibility improvements]) and I explain what I built, what challenged me and what I learned.

What I bring to a team

  • A user-first mindset: years in customer service and product management taught me to understand users' real problems.
  • Autonomy: I've managed clients on my own, from the first brief to delivery.
  • Teaching skills: I've trained clients and students, so I can explain technical choices simply.
  • Modern front-end: React, reusable components, state management and API calls.

I'm looking for my first position within a team, in Paris or remote.
