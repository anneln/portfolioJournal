Why did I start codding ?

After many years in marketing with a strong focus on customer satisfaction, I changed my career direction in 2014.
I completed Le Wagon's intensive bootcamp in Paris to become a Full-stack Ruby Developer. This experience launched my journey into web development.

How do I keep my knowledge up to date ?

Until 2023, I worked for a digital communication agency that built websites with various CMS. Even if I customized them with code, it wasn't enough to stay updated on various languages.
Since then, to maintain my knowledge while working as a freelance, I watched online tutorials, followed courses on different platforms and practiced coding with Exercism or similar tools.
I enrolled in Scrimba's Front End Career Path to improve my coding in HTML5, CSS3, modern JavaScript (ES6+),responsive design, best practices, accessibility and learn React.
